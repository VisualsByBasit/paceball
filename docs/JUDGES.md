# How to try Paceball

Thanks for taking a look. Paceball is in closed testing on Google Play, so it takes a couple of extra taps to install. The whole thing takes about five minutes.

1. **You need an Android phone and a Google account.** Paceball needs Android 9 or newer: it steps through the video frame by frame, and Android only allows that from Android 9 (API 28).

2. **Join the tester Google Group:** <https://groups.google.com/g/paceball-testers>

3. **Opt in to testing.** Open <https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.paceball.app>, signed in with the same Google account you used for the group, and tap "Become a tester".

4. **Install Paceball from Google Play:** <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.paceball.app>
   The listing can take a few minutes to show up after you opt in.

5. **Try it free.** Every phone gets 3 analyses per 7 days, with no payment and no account.

6. **Try Pro.** Open the paywall (Settings > Pro, or any locked feature) and pick the annual plan with the 7-day free trial.
   - Google asks for a payment method, but nothing is charged if you cancel within 7 days.
   - To cancel: Play Store > profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Paceball > Cancel.
   - "Restore purchases" is at the bottom of the paywall, and in Settings > Pro.
   - Prices are the store's own and show in your local currency.

7. **A 2-minute test without a cricket pitch.**
   - Put two markers (shoes or tape) a known distance apart on the floor, for example 3 m.
   - Record someone rolling or throwing a ball past them. Keep the phone still, side-on, with both markers in the frame.
   - On the Mark screen, choose "Two markers" as the ruler and enter the distance. Tap the two markers, then the ball at release and where it bounces.
   - You get the average speed with its ± range.

8. **What to look at.**
   - The speed and its ± range, worked out for that reading.
   - "Marked, not tracked" on the frame: the line joins the points you tapped, nothing more.
   - The share card, Free vs Pro (Share reading on the result).
   - The video clip export, with the reading burned in.
   - Stats (tap the avatar on Home) and History.

9. **Known limitation.** Right after a video clip is exported, the in-app player can report a shorter length (about 1 s). Open it again from History a few minutes later and it shows the full length. The saved file is complete.
